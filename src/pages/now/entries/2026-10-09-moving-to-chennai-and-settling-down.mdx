# Moving to Chennai and settling down

## October 9, 2026

A lot has changed since I set up this website and wrote my first post. In January this year, I moved from Bangalore to Chennai. I'll probably write a post someday about why I moved and what my first experiences of living in Chennai were like.

The first few months were pretty unstructured as we tried to settle in as a family. Things feel much more settled now: my daughter has adjusted to her new school, and we've found a new routine.

Another big change since my last post is that I've enrolled in a master's program in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I'm studying alongside my full-time job, and it's been refreshing and tiring in equal measure. I'm learning a lot, and I'm pleasantly surprised by how much I can fit into a day. It's definitely tiring, though, and lately my sleep has taken a hit. Overall, it's been a good experience so far.
